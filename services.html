Structural Engineering

The Structural Engineering Division is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities for testing construction materials and analyzing structural components. Our expert team provides consultancy for the design, assessment, and retrofitting of various civil engineering structures.

We ensure that all tests adhere to international standards such as ASTM, AASHTO, and BNBC to guarantee the safety and durability of infrastructure projects.

Key Tests

  • Concrete (hardened): cylinders, cubes, flexural beams, core cutting and testing, casting and testing, and density test, etc.
  • Concrete (fresh): slump, density, temperature, moisture content, and air entainment, and flow test, etc.
  • Concrete mix design: with or without admixture, for normal and high strength concrete
  • Cement: compressive strength, normal consistency, setting time, fineness, specific gravity, density, soundness, and bag weight, etc.
  • Reinforcement rods: tension (including weight and elongation), bend, re-bend, stress-strain curves (modulus of elasticity), and shear, etc.
  • Bricks: absorption, crushing strength, size and shape, unit weight, and efflorescence, etc.
  • NDT: rebound hammer, concrete cover, rebar location and diameter, and pile integrity test, etc.
  • Admixture parameters: pH, temperature, density, solid content, water-reducing capability, and slump retention, etc.
  • Calibration: proving rings, electronic balances and platform scales, and rebound hammers, etc.

Key Consultency Services

  • Structural analysis and design of residential, commercial, industrial and institutional buildings
  • Analysis and design of bridges, culverts and other infrastructure structures
  • Building design vetting and structural safety assessment
  • Independent review of structural designs and drawings
  • Structural health monitoring (SHM) and condition assessment of existing structures
  • Preparation for technical specifications for various structural engineering projects
  • Safety assessment of old structures & quality assessment of new constructions
  • Retrofitting of earthquake vulnerable structures

Transportation Engineering

The Transportation Engineering Division supports the planning, design, and quality control of roads, pavements, and railway infrastructure. We combine laboratory testing of pavement materials with consultancy in traffic studies and road safety.

Our testing follows recognized standards such as ASTM and AASHTO to support reliable pavement performance.

Key Tests

  • Aggregates for pavement and rail: gradation of base/sub-base and ballast, ACV, AIV, TFV, L.A.A., flakiness and elongation index, % of fractured particles, and soundness, etc.
  • Base and sub-base materials: standard and modified Proctor tests, California Bearing Ratio (CBR), Dynamic Cone penetration (DCP), etc.
  • Bitumen: specific gravity, density, penetration, ductility, etc.
  • Asphalt mixes: Marshall stability and flow, bitumen content, aggregate gradation of the mix, etc.
  • Pavement field tests: asphalt core thickness, core compressive strength, in-situ core cutting & testing, pavement surface texture, etc.

Key Consultency Services

  • Geometric design of roads and highways
  • Structural design and evaluation of flexible and rigid pavements
  • Traffic studies and surveys (volume, origin-destination, speed, travel time, delay and parking)
  • Traffic forecasting and transportation demand analysis
  • Transportation Impact Assessment (TIA) of development projects
  • Road accident investigation, road safety assessment and road safety auditing
  • Vetting and independent review of designs for roads, bridges, culverts, towers and runways
  • Professional training in traffic studies, traffic management, transportation planning and road safety

Geotechnical Engineering

The Geotechnical Engineering Division characterizes soil behavior through laboratory testing and field investigation, providing the data needed for safe and economical foundation design.

Our services range from basic index tests to sub-soil exploration with complete reporting.

Key Tests

  • Physical and index properties: specific gravity, density/unit weight, void ratio, moisture content, liquid limit, plastic limit and plasticity index, sieve and hydrometer analysis, organic matter content, etc.
  • Compaction and density: standard Proctor, modified Proctor, maximum and minimum density of cohesionless soil, etc.
  • Permeability: cohesive soil (by one-dimensional consolidation), cohesionless soil (falling head and constant head), etc.
  • Strength and deformation: unconfined compression, laboratory California Bearing Ratio (CBR), etc.
  • Direct shear: consolidated drained tests for sand and clay, etc.
  • Field tests: field dry density (FDD), pile integrity test, etc.

Key Consultency Services

  • Geotechnical analysis and design of shallow and deep foundations
  • Design and analysis of embankments & earth retaining structures
  • In-situ pile characterization using Pile Integrity Tests (PIT)
  • Planning and design of soil improvement schemes
  • Sub-soil exploration
